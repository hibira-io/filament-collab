# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- `CollaborativeEditor`: a rich text field several people can edit at once,
  with live cursors and avatars. Yjs documents are stored in your database
  and relayed over Hibira; changes too large for one event are fetched.
- `HibiraCollabPlugin` adds the document routes and presence channel to a
  panel.
- English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, ไทย and Tiếng Việt.
